AUDIOBOOK ACX

Narration for audiobooks: noise reduction, de-essing, a voice EQ, gentle compression and a true-peak limiter at -3.5 dB, with the loudness meter to keep the level between the ACX limits (RMS -23 to -18 dB, peaks at -3 dB or below).

Load it

  1. Install NULL JSFX with the Hub or the JSFX download: the chains come in its FXChains folder.
  2. In REAPER, open a track's FX window and pick FX → Add FX chain, then NULL JSFX (REAPER's sliders) or NULL JSFX GFX (our interfaces), then Audiobook ACX.
  3. In any other DAW: load the NULL JSFX Rack (VST3 or CLAP) and pick its preset Audiobook ACX.
