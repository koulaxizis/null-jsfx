Remote for Mixers controls a digital mixer from your Android phone or tablet over the mixer's Wi-Fi or your local network. It works with Behringer X AIR mixers (XR12, XR16, XR18, X18) with the Behringer X32 and Midas M32 families (X32, Compact, Producer, Rack, Core; M32, M32R, M32C) with the Behringer WING (WING, Compact, Rack; no meters yet) and with the Allen & Heath SQ (SQ-5, SQ-6, SQ-7: faders, mutes, pan, sends and mute groups; its protocol has no names, meters or solo).

This is a beta: it is tested against software X AIR, X32, WING and SQ consoles. Please report anything that behaves differently on your mixer.

<b>What it does</b>
<ul>
<li>Finds the mixer on the network and recognises its model by itself, with step-by-step connection help. You can also type its IP address.</li>
<li>Channels, aux, FX returns, buses, FX sends, DCAs and the main mix: faders, mute, solo, pan and meters, with names and colours from the mixer.</li>
<li>Sends on faders: pick a bus or FX and the faders become the sends to it, for monitor mixes.</li>
<li>Mute groups and Clear solo.</li>
<li>My mix: lock a musician's phone to the sends of their own monitor bus, without mute or solo.</li>
<li>Screen lock that opens only with a long press, so a stray touch on stage changes nothing.</li>
<li>Faders move with your finger from where they are; they never jump to where you touch.</li>
</ul>
