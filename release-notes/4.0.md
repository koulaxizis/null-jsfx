## 4.0: 14 new plugins, a bigger Rack, 2,040 presets

- **14 NEW PLUGINS** (beta): Phase Distortion, Lo-Fi Comp, Spectral Blur, Bloom Delay, Null Test, Key Detect, MIDI Monitor, Strum, two new instruments **CHOIR** and **WIND** (flute, clarinet, saxophone, brass), and four One Slider plugins: Echo One, Warmer, Closer, Smoother
- **RACK**: lanes A to D, a split into up to 5 bands with solo and bypass, a MIDI input, new modulators (Smooth Random, a Follower of a slot's output, a pitch follower, an ADSR, MIDI sources), a Remap curve on every route, and Scenes with Morph; 20 new Rack templates
- **PRESETS**: 2,040 factory presets (up to 32 per plugin), presets per use (vocals, drums, instruments, bus and master), and the old presets level-matched to their Default
- **NEW FEATURES**: a drawn Custom shape in Volume Shaper, Talkbox in Vocoder, Morph and Nonlinear in Filter, your own wavetables in Synth, Generate in Trance Gate and Step FX, and a hint line under the controls
- **FX CHAINS** for REAPER: Audiobook ACX, Broadcast −23 LUFS, Mix Check, Acid Line, Arp Pluck, Chord Pad, Lo-Fi Keys, Hum to Synth, Strummed Guitar
- **NULL JSFX HUB**: starter profiles (Podcast, Beatmaking, Mixing, Mastering, Guitar, Sound Design) install a ready set in one click
- **SITE**: play a loop through a plugin right on its page (most effects)
- With their default settings the existing plugins sound as in 3.3.1; the new options are off until you turn them on. A few factory presets are quieter or louder than before, so that they match Default
