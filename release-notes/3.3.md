## 3.3: Steadier remotes

- **REMOTE FOR MIXERS** (beta): the X32, M32 and X AIR read every fader, send and setting on connect, also over a slow or lossy network, and read them again after a dropout; the link no longer drops when a single packet is lost; Scan finds mixers with a VPN app on
- WING, Allen & Heath and Yamaha: steadier links; a connection that dies silently (Wi-Fi gone, a VPN reconnecting) is noticed and reconnected, with everything read again
- **REMOTE FOR DAWS**: Bitwig, Ableton Live, Cubase and FL Studio notice a dead link and reconnect; Ardour and Mixbus ask once more for what a lossy network dropped
- **HARMONIZER**: a Sustain Pedal option for Mode MIDI (off by default)
- The plugins sound exactly as in 3.2

Using a VPN app on your phone (Proton, NordVPN, WireGuard…)? Exclude Remote for Mixers in its split tunnelling, or allow LAN connections.
