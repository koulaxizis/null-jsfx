## 4.1: 6 new plugins, generative tools, the dice

- **6 NEW PLUGINS** (beta): **STEP FILTER** (a filter that follows up to 16 steps, with glide and a seed), **BEAT GEN** (eight-lane drum patterns as MIDI, with lock and mutate), **BAND SPIN** (four bands, each on its own rotor), **HIT LAYER** (a tuned tone and noise under every hit), **BASS PRESENCE** (One Slider: the lows heard on small speakers) and the instrument **MUSIC BOX**
- **THE DICE**: a die in the header of every plugin moves its controls a little (Shift: anywhere), as one undo step; Mix, Output and the like never move
- **GENERATIVE**: Mutate, Held Chord and Legato in Riff; Generate and Auto Advance in Chords; Flow and Mutate in Trance Gate; patterns in Auto Pan; Reroll and Speed Chance in Slicer
- **NEW FEATURES**: separate detect and suppress bands in DeEsser, dynamic boosts in Passive EQ, Azimuth and Dropouts in Tape, Speed Follow in Rotary Speaker, a high band in Stereo Width
- **FX CHAINS** for REAPER: Generative Beat, Generative Riff, Chord Jam; **RACK** templates: Sequenced Filter, Sequenced Panner, Looped Envelopes; new presets for all of the above
- **LOGO**: the mirrored N everywhere, in the plugin windows too
- With their default settings the existing plugins sound as in 4.0; the new options are off until you turn them on
