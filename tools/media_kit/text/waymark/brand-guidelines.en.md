# Brand guidelines: Waymark

Short rules for anyone who uses the Waymark logos: users, writers, reviewers, printers.

## The mark

A white map pin in a pine-green circle (`logos/svg/waymark-icon.svg`, the official mark). Without the circle, the pin stands on its own in green (`#5ca89e` on dark backgrounds) or in white or black.

| | |
|---|---|
| Brand colour | pine green `#01796F` (palette v1: `#198378`, `#066c64`, `#5ca89e`) |
| Background | near-black `#090e0e`, surface `#1a2120` |
| Font | Inter (or the system UI font) |
| Tagline | "Tools that keep the show on track." |

Full palette with HEX, RGB and approximate CMYK is in `palettes/` (`.ase`, `.gpl`, `.css`, `.json`, `.png`).

## Name

- **Waymark** in running text; the logo sets it in capitals, letter-spaced: WAYMARK.
- Tools are named "Waymark" plus the tool, for example "Waymark Remote".

## Clear space and size

- Keep empty space around a logo at least as wide as half the icon's height.
- Smallest size: icon 24 px on screen or 8 mm in print; horizontal logo 120 px or 30 mm wide.

## Backgrounds

- The colour logos are made for dark backgrounds. On light backgrounds use the `black` single-colour files.
- On photos or busy backgrounds use the `white` or `black` single-colour files.

## Please do not

- recolour, stretch, rotate, outline or add shadows or glows to the mark;
- rebuild the wordmark in another font;
- describe Waymark as released, beta or stable, or link to downloads: it is in design and has none yet;
- use the logos in a way that suggests the project endorses you or your product;
- sell the logos or wallpapers, or products made mostly from them.
