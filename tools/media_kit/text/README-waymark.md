# Waymark media kit

Free logos, wallpapers, banners, print files, palettes, fonts and press texts.

Waymark is in design: there is no release and nothing to download yet.

- `wallpapers/`: dark, three styles (`mark`, `pattern`, `minimal`), sizes 4K, 5K, 16:10, ultrawide, Android, iPhone and tablet (JPEG).
- `logos/`: icon (512–4096 px), single-colour pin marks, horizontal and stacked logos for dark backgrounds, plus white and black single-colour versions (PNG, transparent), and `svg/` sources (`waymark-icon.svg` is the official mark).
- `banners/`: Open Graph, X header, YouTube, Facebook, LinkedIn (profile and company page), GitHub social preview, avatar, and the site's own social image (PNG).
- `print/`: Printful-ready t-shirt, mug and sticker files (see `print/README.md`).
- `palettes/`: palette v1 (dark theme and brand greens) as `.ase`, `.gpl`, `.css`, `.json` and a swatch card.
- `fonts/`: Inter with its OFL licence.
- `press/`: descriptions (short, medium, long), facts sheet and an announcement, in English and Greek.
- `brand-guidelines.en.md` / `.el.md` and `LICENSE-media-kit.md`.
