# Brand guidelines: NULL JSFX, orOS, noxpress

Short rules for anyone who uses these logos: users, writers, reviewers, printers.

## The three marks

| Project | Mark | Main colour | Background | Font |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| NULL JSFX | silver "N" stroke in a dark rounded square | silver `#c4c4c4` | near-black `#1b1a18` | Noto Sans |
| orOS | gold mountain outline | gold `#d4af37` | warm black `#14120d` | Nunito (Greek: M PLUS Rounded 1c) |
| noxpress | violet arch "n", wordmark "noxpress." | violet `#6d4aff` | ink `#0d0f14` | Inter |

Full palettes with HEX, RGB and approximate CMYK are in `palettes/` (`.ase`, `.gpl`, `.css`, `.json`, `.png`).

## Names

- **NULL JSFX** in capitals. Plugin names in a DAW have no prefix (for example "Tilt"); the maker is "NULL JSFX".
- **orOS**: small "or", capital "OS".
- **noxpress**: all lower case; the violet full stop belongs to the wordmark.

## Clear space and size

- Keep empty space around a logo at least as wide as half the icon's height.
- Smallest size: icon 24 px on screen or 8 mm in print; horizontal logo 120 px or 30 mm wide.

## Backgrounds

- The colour logos are made for dark backgrounds. On light backgrounds use the `black` single-colour files.
- On photos or busy backgrounds use the `white` or `black` single-colour files.

## Please do not

- recolour, stretch, rotate, outline or add shadows or glows to the marks;
- rebuild the wordmarks in another font;
- put the violet of noxpress on NULL JSFX material on its own (the three colours meet only in the combined "all three" files);
- use the logos in a way that suggests the projects endorse you or your product;
- sell the logos or wallpapers, or products made mostly from them.
