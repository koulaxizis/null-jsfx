# noxpress: 32 free WordPress and WooCommerce plugins, managed from one hub

**For immediate release · 2 November 2026**

noxpress is a collection of free, open-source (MIT) WordPress and WooCommerce plugins for shops and publishers, built to replace paid third-party plugins: revenue splitting with per-product VAT, store dashboards, shop filters, bundles, pre-orders, wishlists, checkout extras, store pickup, page caching, image optimisation, redirects, backups and more.

- **One hub:** install, update and remove every plugin from a single screen, with signed updates straight from noxpress.tech.
- **One look:** a calm, dark, minimal admin shared by the whole suite, while the shop front follows the site's own theme.
- **No catches:** no ads, upsells, licence keys, nags or tracking.
- **On the go:** Android companion apps for Site Bridge and Live Operator.

> "A small shop deserves the same tools as a big one, without a monthly bill for every feature." (Christos Koulaxizis, creator)
