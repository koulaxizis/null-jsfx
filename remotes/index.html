Remote for DAWs beta

Mixer

Your DAW's mixer and transport

Faders, pan, mute, solo and arm with meters, and the transport, for the DAW on your computer.

  • REAPER, Ardour and Mixbus, Bitwig Studio, Ableton Live, Cubase and FL Studio
  • Finds REAPER on your network; Cubase and FL Studio over network MIDI
Plugins

The plugin's own interface

Open a track's FX and every NULL JSFX plugin shows the interface it has on the computer, with its presets and A/B. What you turn moves in the DAW.

Sends

Sends and FX tracks

Set the sends of a track to its FX tracks, as in the DAW.

Mackie Control · beta

Any DAW with Mackie Control

Mixer, transport, meters and time display for Logic Pro, Studio One, Pro Tools, Cakewalk, Reason and other DAWs with Mackie Control, without plugins.

Remote for Mixers beta

Consoles

Your digital mixer

It finds the console on your network and knows its model. Channels, buses, FX returns and main: faders, mute, solo, pan and meters.

  • Behringer X AIR, X32 and WING, Midas M32
  • Allen & Heath SQ, Qu, CQ, dLive and Avantis; Yamaha CL, QL, TF and DM7
My mix

A musician's own monitor mix

Lock the app to one bus and hand it to the drummer: they set their own monitor mix and nothing else.

  • Monitor sends, DCAs and mute groups
Any console

One app, many desks

Allen & Heath and Yamaha desks connect over the network too, with the same faders and mutes.

Remote for orOS Studio

orOS Studio from your phone

Control orOS Studio on your computer while the sound plays there. Scan the QR code Studio shows and you are in.

  • Every NULL JSFX effect with its own interface, presets and A/B
  • Pads and keys for the NULL instruments, recorded into MIDI clips
Get the APK Or in a browser

Three steps

  1. InstallThe APK from here, F-Droid, Obtainium or Koulaxizis Apps.
  2. ConnectPhone and computer or mixer on the same Wi-Fi: it finds REAPER and the consoles, or you type the address.
  3. MixFrom the stage, the booth or the couch.
