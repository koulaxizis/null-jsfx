Privacy

What NULL JSFX, its website and its apps do with your data: almost nothing.

In short

NULL JSFX has no accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. We do not collect personal data about you, and the plugins themselves never connect to the internet.

The website, the downloads and the update checks are served by GitHub, so GitHub sees your IP address, as any web server does. This page explains where that happens and what you can do about it.

Who is responsible

Christos Koulaxizis, Greece, who makes NULL JSFX. Write to koulaxizis@glarolykoi.net about anything on this page.

Full legal details (tax number and registry) are on koulaxizis.gr.

This website

nulljsfx.tech is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). When you open a page, GitHub receives your IP address and browser details and keeps them in its own logs for security. We cannot see those logs. The fonts come from your own computer, and nothing is loaded from other companies.

The videos and the downloads are files on our GitHub releases. A video loads only when you press play, and a file only when you download it.

What your browser keeps

There are no cookies. The site keeps four small settings in your browser's local storage so that it remembers your choices. They never leave your device:

  • nulljsfx-theme: dark or light theme.
  • nulljsfx-lang: English or Greek, on the pages that have both.
  • nulljsfx-support-seen: when the support note before a download last showed, so that it shows at most once in 30 days.
  • nulljsfx-support-off: that you chose “Don't show this again”.

You can delete them at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

The apps

The plugins (JSFX, VST3, CLAP, LV2, Rack) make no network connection at all.

NULL JSFX Hub downloads the release list from GitHub once a day, and the plugin files from GitHub when you install or update. GitHub sees your IP address, as with any download. The Hub sends no account, no identifier and no list of what you have installed.

Remote for DAWs and Remote for Mixers (Android) talk only to the computers and mixers on your own network that you pick. The addresses you save stay on your phone. To tell you about a new version, the apps ask GitHub's API for the latest release, which shows GitHub your IP address. They ask only for network access (Internet, network and Wi-Fi state, and multicast to find devices), and they are not backed up to the cloud. If you install them from our F-Droid repository or through Obtainium, those apps read our repository on GitHub too.

The tablet remote for REAPER is off unless you install it, and then it works only on your own network.

When you write to us

If you email us, we use your address and message only to answer you, and we delete them at the latest one year after our last exchange. Issues and comments on GitHub are public and are kept by GitHub under its own terms.

Donations go through Viva's payment page and merchandise is sold by the Glarolykoi shop. Each has its own privacy policy. We never see your card details.

Legal basis and transfers

Serving the site and checking for updates rely on our legitimate interest in delivering free software safely (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)); answering your email relies on the same interest. GitHub, Inc. is in the United States; its Privacy Statement explains how it protects data that leaves the EU.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete what we hold about you, or object to its use, by writing to koulaxizis@glarolykoi.net. We answer within one month. You can also complain to the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (www.dpa.gr).
