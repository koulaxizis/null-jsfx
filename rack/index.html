The tour

Chain

Eight slots, any order

Pick any of 123 modules for each slot: the same DSP as the NULL JSFX plugins, so a module sounds exactly like its plugin. Drag a slot to reorder, bypass it or set its own mix.

  • Every module with its own interface
  • A/B, undo and presets per module
  • 112 factory presets in three groups
Routing

Bands, Mid/Side, left and right

Put a slot on the lows, the mids or the highs at crossovers you choose, split into up to five bands, or work on mid, side, left or right alone.

  • Low-Mid and Mid-High crossovers on the front panel
  • Split into 2 to 5 bands, with solo and bypass per band
Modulation

Macros that move many controls

Eight macros, each with as many routes as you need. Turn one knob and a build-up rises, a filter opens and the pump drops in, all at once.

  • Up to 32 routes, each with its depth and curve
  • Scenes: up to 4 snapshots that Scene Morph moves through
Movement

LFOs and an envelope follower

Two LFOs with tempo sync and five shapes, an envelope follower that listens to the input, Smooth Random, a pitch follower and an ADSR.

  • Sine, triangle, square, saw and random
  • Sources from MIDI: note, velocity, gate, mod wheel, pressure, bend, CC
Rhythm

A 16-step sequencer

Steps with glide, from 1/32 to whole notes, locked to the host's tempo: rhythmic gates, filter patterns and stutters.

Lanes

Parallel lanes and a feedback loop

Run up to four lanes side by side with their own gain, pan, mute and solo, or feed the output of one slot back into an earlier one.

  • Lane Mix between lanes A and B
  • Feedback from any slot to any earlier slot
