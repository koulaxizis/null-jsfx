VIBRATO

Delay-Line Pitch Vibrato

Download free Part of NULL JSFX 2.7: 126 plugins, free and open source (MIT). Easiest with NULL JSFX Hub.

Listen

The same music with the Vocal Vibrato preset and without the plugin. Switch while it plays.

Controls

  • Rate0.1 to 15 Hz — Speed of the sine LFO.
  • Depth0 to 20 ms — Delay sweep range, from 0.1 ms to 0.1 ms + Depth; more depth means wider pitch swing.
  • Mix0 to 100% — Blend between dry and modulated signal (100% for pure vibrato, lower for chorus-like detune).

Best uses

  • Vibrato on vocals and leads
  • Tape-style warble
  • Chorus-like detune at partial mix
  • Lo-fi wobble effects

Properties

Input → modulated delay line (0.1 ms to 0.1 ms + Depth) with linear interpolation → Dry/Wet Mix → Output. One LFO drives both channels; zero latency.

Formats

  • JSFXJSFX for REAPER, with REAPER's own sliders
  • GFXJSFX GFX for REAPER, with its own interface
  • VST3VST3 for any DAW
  • CLAPCLAP for any DAW
