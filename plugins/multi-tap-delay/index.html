MULTI-TAP DELAY

Rhythmic Multi-Tap Echo

Download free Part of NULL JSFX 3.0: 150 plugins, free and open source (MIT). Easiest with NULL JSFX Hub.

Controls

  • Taps1 to 8 — How many echoes (1 to 8).
  • PatternEven / Dotted / Triplet / Swing / Speed Up / Slow Down / Scatter — Where the echoes fall: even steps, dotted, triplet, swing, speeding up, slowing down or scattered.
  • Time10 to 1000 ms — The step between echoes in milliseconds (when Tempo Sync is off).
  • Tempo SyncOff / On — Lock the step to the host tempo.
  • Subdivision1/4 / 1/8 / 1/16 / 1/32 / 1/2 — The note value of the step when synced.
  • Decay0 to 100% — How much quieter each echo is than the one before.
  • Spread0 to 100% — How far the echoes are panned left and right in turn.
  • Feedback0 to 90% — Sends the last echo back into the line for longer tails.
  • Low Cut20 to 2000 Hz — High-pass on the echoes.
  • High Cut1000 to 20000 Hz — Low-pass on the echoes.
  • Mix0 to 100% — Blend between the dry signal and the echoes.

Best uses

  • Rhythmic echoes on vocals and guitars
  • Bouncing-ball and slowing-down effects
  • Wide stereo patterns on synths
  • Dub-style repeats with feedback

Properties

Input → 4 s stereo line → up to 8 taps at pattern × step (Time or a synced note), level (1 − Decay)^(k−1), alternating pan by Spread → sum → 12 dB/oct low cut and high cut → Dry/Wet Mix; the last tap feeds back. Zero latency.
