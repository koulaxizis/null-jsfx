BAND SPIN BETA

Four Bands, Four Rotors

Controls

  • Crossover 130 to 1000 Hz — Crossover between the low and the low-mid band
  • Crossover 2200 to 6000 Hz — Crossover between the low-mid and the high-mid band
  • Crossover 31000 to 16000 Hz — Crossover between the high-mid and the high band
  • Speed 10.05 to 10 Hz — Turns per second of the low band's rotor
  • Speed 20.05 to 10 Hz — Turns per second of the low-mid band's rotor
  • Speed 30.05 to 10 Hz — Turns per second of the high-mid band's rotor
  • Speed 40.05 to 10 Hz — Turns per second of the high band's rotor
  • Volume0 to 100% — How much a band dips as its rotor turns away
  • Pan0 to 100% — How far a band swings left and right
  • Doppler0 to 100% — How much a band's pitch bends as its rotor comes and goes (up to 1 ms of delay)
  • Follow0 to 100% — How much each rotor speeds up with its band's level (up to three times its Speed)
  • Mix0 to 100% — Blend with the bands summed without motion (in phase, so a partial Mix never combs)

Best uses

  • Movement on pads and keys, a different speed in each band
  • Wide, swirling guitars
  • Highs that spin while the lows stay put
  • Drum buses that move more when they hit harder

Properties

Signal chain: Input → Linkwitz-Riley 4th-order crossover into four bands (as in Band Splitter, with allpasses that keep them in phase) → per band a rotor: constant-power pan, a volume dip and a Doppler delay (linear interpolation) → summed → Mix → Output. The rotors have 0.3 s of inertia; Follow uses an envelope per band (10 ms up, 300 ms down). Stereo, zero latency.
