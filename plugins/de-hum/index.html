DE-HUM

Mains Hum Notch Remover

Download free Part of NULL JSFX 2.7: 113 plugins, free and open source (MIT). Easiest with NULL JSFX Hub.

Listen

The same music with the Mains Clean 50 Hz preset and without the plugin. Switch while it plays.

Controls

  • Frequency50 Hz / 60 Hz — Mains frequency of the hum: 50 Hz (Europe, most of the world) or 60 Hz (Americas, parts of Asia)
  • Harmonics1 to 8 — Number of notches: the fundamental and its multiples (1 = fundamental only, 8 = up to the 8th harmonic)
  • Q5 to 100 — Notch width as Q: bandwidth = frequency / Q, so higher values cut narrower and leave more of the music
  • Depth0 to 60 dB — How deep each notch cuts, from 0 (off) to 60 dB
  • Fine−2 to +2 Hz — Fine tune of the mains frequency in Hz (±2), for hum that sits slightly off 50/60 Hz; the harmonics follow
  • Low CutOff / 20 Hz / 30 Hz / 40 Hz — Optional 24 dB/oct high-pass below the hum (20, 30 or 40 Hz) to clear rumble

Best uses

  • Remove ground-loop hum from guitar, bass and DI recordings
  • Clean buzz from field recordings, interviews and podcasts
  • Repair old tapes and vinyl transfers with mains hum
  • Tidy hum on vintage synths and noisy amp recordings
  • Clear subsonic rumble along with the hum

Properties

Signal chain: Input → optional Low Cut (two TPT high-pass stages, 24 dB/oct) → up to 8 notch biquads at h × (50/60 Hz + Fine) → Output. Each notch has the poles of a band of width f/Q and cuts by exactly Depth at its centre; stereo, zero latency.
