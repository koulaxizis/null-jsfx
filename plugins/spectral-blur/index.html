SPECTRAL BLUR BETA

Spectral Smear, Shift and Gate

Beta: its rules have been tested on test signals so far and may still be tuned from your feedback, so a project saved now can sound a little different after an update.

Controls

  • Smear0 to 100% — How long each frequency lingers: rising bins follow within up to 150 ms, fading bins hang on for up to 3 s, so a soft spectral tail grows out of the sound
  • Shift−2000 to +2000 Hz — Moves every frequency up or down by the same number of Hz: an inharmonic, metallic frequency shift
  • Tilt−6 to +6 dB/oct — Leans the spectrum around 1 kHz, in dB per octave (flat below 100 Hz and above 10 kHz): negative is darker, positive brighter
  • Gate−90 to 0 dB — Pushes down every frequency bin quieter than this level (a 1:3 expander per bin); at -90 it is off
  • Mix0 to 100% — Blend with the dry signal, which is delayed to match
  • Output−24 to +12 dB — Output level

Best uses

  • Soft spectral tails and washes on vocals, guitars and keys
  • Pads and drones from any sound
  • Metallic, robotic or bell-like tones with Shift
  • Spectral gating: only the strongest partials survive
  • Sound design and transitions

Properties

Input → 2048-point FFT, Hann windows, 4x overlap → each bin shifted by Shift (rounded to the bin spacing, phase kept coherent) and tilted around 1 kHz (flat below 100 Hz and above 10 kHz) → magnitude smoothed over time (Smear: up to 150 ms rising, 3 s falling; fading bins keep turning with a little Park-Miller random drift) → per-bin 1:3 expander below Gate → inverse FFT and overlap-add → mix with the delayed dry signal → Output gain.
