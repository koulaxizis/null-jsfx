FORMANT SHIFT

Vocal Formant Morph

Download free Part of NULL JSFX 2.7: 113 plugins, free and open source (MIT). Easiest with NULL JSFX Hub.

Listen

The same music with the Giant Voice preset and without the plugin. Switch while it plays.

Controls

  • Formant Position0 to 100 — Formant position with 50 as bypass: moving left lowers the three resonances (down to 0.5x) for a deeper, larger character, moving right raises them (up to 2x) for a smaller, brighter one, with the filtered blend growing toward both ends (max 80% wet)

Best uses

  • Gender-bend vocal character
  • Make backing vocals sound like a different singer
  • Creative vowel colouring on synths
  • Robotic or cartoon voice effects
  • Subtle vocal timbre change

Properties

Input → three parallel band-pass resonators (base 300 / 850 / 2250 Hz, Q 2.5) scaled by the shift factor → weighted sum blended with dry → Output. Not a pitch shifter: only the resonant colour moves; zero latency.
