MONO MAKER

Low-End Mono Summing

Download free Part of NULL JSFX 2.8: 133 plugins, free and open source (MIT). Easiest with NULL JSFX Hub.

Controls

  • Amount0 to 100 — Crossover frequency below which the side signal is removed: 0 is off, 100 makes everything under 400 Hz mono (exponential 20–400 Hz sweep)

Best uses

  • Centre the bass on mix and master buses
  • Fix wide, phasey low end from stereo synths
  • Vinyl-safe low frequencies
  • Tighten kick and bass in stereo samples
  • Keep highs wide while lows stay mono

Properties

Input → M/S split → side signal through a 4th-order low-pass (two Butterworth stages) → low side removed from L/R → Output. Mid and high side content are untouched; zero latency.

Formats

  • JSFXJSFX for REAPER, with REAPER's own sliders
  • GFXJSFX GFX for REAPER, with its own interface
  • VST3VST3 for any DAW
  • CLAPCLAP for any DAW
  • LV2LV2 for Linux DAWs such as Ardour, Carla and Qtractor
