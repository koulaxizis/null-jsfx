CONSOLE CHANNEL

Console Channel Colour

Download free Part of NULL JSFX 2.8: 133 plugins, free and open source (MIT). Easiest with NULL JSFX Hub.

Controls

  • CharacterClean / Warm / Punchy / Bright — Clean: no emphasis, symmetric saturation. Warm: the lows saturate first, with even harmonics. Punchy: the deep lows saturate first, with more drive. Bright: the highs saturate first.
  • Drive0 to 100% — How hard the channel is driven: up to 12, 15 or 18 dB depending on the character; small signals stay clean.
  • Output−12 to +12 dB — Output level after the console stage.

Best uses

  • Analog-desk colour on every track of a mix
  • Warming up thin digital sources
  • Adding weight and punch to drums and bass
  • Use with Console Buss on the bus the tracks feed

Properties

Input → first-order pre-emphasis shelf (per character) → soft saturator (Padé tanh, 6 dB of headroom, even harmonics for Warm and Punchy) → the exact inverse shelf (flat response at low level) → 5 Hz DC blocker → Output. Zero latency, stereo channels processed independently.

Formats

  • CLAPCLAP for any DAW
