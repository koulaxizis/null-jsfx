CHOIR BETA

A Synthetic Choir With Vowels

Beta: its rules have been tested on test signals so far and may still be tuned from your feedback, so a project saved now can sound a little different after an update.

Download free Part of NULL JSFX 4.1.1: 174 plugins, free and open source (MIT). Easiest with NULL JSFX Hub.

Controls

  • Vowel0 to 4 — The vowel sung: A, E, I, O, U and every blend between (the mod wheel moves it towards U)
  • Voices1 to 8 — How many singers each key has, from a soloist to a section of eight
  • Detune0 to 40 ct — How far the singers of a key are spread around its pitch
  • Breath0 to 100% — Breath noise in each singer's voice, pulsed with its vocal cords
  • Vibrato0 to 100% — Depth of the singers' vibrato, which fades in a quarter second after the key
  • Vibrato Rate3 to 8 Hz — Speed of the vibrato; every singer is a little faster or slower
  • SectionAuto / Soprano / Alto / Tenor / Bass — Auto lets each key sing in the voices nearest its pitch; the others pick soprano, alto, tenor or bass
  • Attack5 to 3000 ms — How slowly the keys swell in
  • Release20 to 5000 ms — How long the keys ring after they are let go
  • Brightness0 to 100% — The vocal tone, from dark to bright, before the formants
  • Velocity0 to 100% — How much velocity changes the level and the tone; 0% sings every key at full velocity
  • Stereo0 to 100% — How far the singers spread across the stereo field
  • MPEOff / On — On: pitch bend, pressure and CC74 timbre of channels 2-16 belong to the key played there
  • MPE Bend Range0 to 96 st — Pitch bend range of the MPE channels
  • Volume−24 to +12 dB — Output level

Best uses

  • Aah and Ooh pads under a song or a film cue
  • A soft solo voice line, or a bass section humming the roots
  • Vowel sweeps from the mod wheel for movement in a held chord
  • Expressive voices from an MPE controller

Properties

MIDI in → every key sings with 1 to 8 singers (PolyBLEP sawtooths, each with its own detune, slow drift and delayed vibrato, plus breath noise pulsed by its glottal cycle, by an exact Park-Miller step), spread across the stereo field; a one-pole glottal tilt per key (darker when soft), then seven banks of five formant resonators per channel (soprano, alto, tenor, bass and three between, for the vowel A-E-I-O-U and its blends). Auto sends a key to the two banks nearest its pitch. One attack/release envelope per key, sustain pedal, mod wheel to the vowel, pitch bend +-2 st, MPE bend, pressure and timbre. No samples. Every MIDI message passes on.
