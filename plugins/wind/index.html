Flute, Clarinet, Sax and Brass by Physics

Controls

  • ModelFlute / Clarinet / Saxophone / Brass — The instrument: Flute, Clarinet, Saxophone or Brass, each a waveguide model with its own range
  • Breath SourceVelocity / Mod Wheel / Breath CC 2 / Aftertouch / Expression CC 11 — What drives the breath: each note's velocity, or a controller (mod wheel, breath CC 2, aftertouch, expression CC 11)
  • Breath0 to 100% — How hard the player blows at the source's full value
  • Noise0 to 100% — The air in the breath; 50% is the model's own, 0% a pure tone
  • Tone0 to 100% — A low-pass on the output, from muffled to fully open
  • Vibrato0 to 100% — Depth of the breath vibrato
  • Vibrato Rate3 to 8 Hz — Speed of the vibrato
  • Vibrato Delay0 to 2000 ms — How long after a new note the vibrato begins to fade in
  • Attack1 to 1000 ms — How quickly the breath comes in at a key
  • Release5 to 2000 ms — How quickly the breath stops after the last key is let go
  • Glide0 to 500 ms — Time to slide to a key played while another is held
  • Volume−24 to +12 dB — Output level

Best uses

  • Solo flute, clarinet, sax or trumpet lines from a keyboard
  • Expressive playing with a breath controller, the mod wheel or aftertouch
  • Legato leads that glide between held notes
  • Soft wind pads and textures under a mix

Properties

MIDI in → one voice (the latest key held; a key over another glides to it). Digital waveguides after STK: Flute (a bore of two periods, a jet a quarter of it, cubic jet, high-passed air noise), Clarinet (a bore of half a period, reed table), Saxophone (one period in two delays, the reed between, a narrow pressure range so it holds its register), Brass (a bore and a lip resonator, tuned per note); each bore length set exactly for its pitch (tools/tune_wind.js keeps it within 12 cents at 44.1, 48 and 96 kHz). Breath from velocity or a controller, with attack, release, vibrato after a delay and noise by an exact Park-Miller step; notes outside a model's range sound an octave in. No samples. Every MIDI message passes on.
