Best uses

  • Electric pianos with chorus, from soft to barking
  • Tubular, glass and digital bells
  • Punchy FM and slap basses
  • Brass stabs, clavs and organ tones
  • Marimba, vibraphone and harp plucks
  • Soft and bell-like pads

Properties

An instrument: MIDI in, stereo out, no samples, 8 voices (a new note takes a silent voice, then the oldest released one, then the oldest; a sounding voice keeps its envelopes and phases, so nothing clicks). Four sine operators per voice in phase modulation, each with a ratio, a linear-attack exponential ADSR and velocity sensitivity; a modulator's level is its index (square law, up to 8 radians), operator 4's feedback averages its last two outputs for stability. A voice ends when every carrier's envelope has. Sample-accurate MIDI with sustain pedal, mod wheel vibrato (5.5 Hz), pitch bend and all notes off, then chorus and an eight-line feedback delay network reverb. The same in the JSFX, the GFX version and the VST3/CLAP.
