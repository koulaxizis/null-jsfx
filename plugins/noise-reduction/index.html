NOISE REDUCTION

Spectral Noise Reducer

Controls

  • LearnOff / On — While On, averages the noise per frequency into a profile: play a noise-only passage, then switch Off
  • AutoOff / On — Tracks the noise floor continuously (minimum statistics) instead of a learnt profile
  • Reduction0 to 60 dB — Maximum attenuation of the noise (the gain floor)
  • Threshold−10 to +20 dB — How far above the noise estimate a sound must rise to pass untouched
  • Smoothing0 to 100% — Smoothing over time and across frequency against musical noise artefacts
  • Attack0 to 100 ms — How fast the gain opens when a sound rises above the noise
  • Release10 to 1000 ms — How fast the gain closes back to the floor
  • ListenOutput / Removed noise — Hear the cleaned output or only the removed noise
  • Mix0 to 100% — Dry/wet blend

Best uses

  • Remove tape and preamp hiss
  • Clean room tone and air-conditioning rumble from dialogue
  • Tame a hum or buzz bed under guitars
  • Lower the noise floor of field and home recordings

Properties

Signal chain: Input → 2048-Point FFT (4× overlap, Hann) → Noise Estimate (Learnt Profile or Auto Minimum Statistics) → Wiener-Like Gain with Floor → Time + Frequency Smoothing → Attack/Release → IFFT Overlap-Add → Mix → Output. One gain for both channels; latency 2047 samples (reported). The learnt profile lives in memory and is not saved with the project: learn again after a reload, or use Auto.
