Best uses

  • Fresh drum ideas for any NULL or third-party drum instrument
  • Evolving grooves that change every few bars but keep the kick steady
  • Polymetric percussion layers
  • A quick beat to write over

Properties

MIDI effect: 8 lanes of up to 16 steps drawn from a style's weights, the lane's Density and Seed with an exact Park-Miller generator (two draws per step: hit and velocity), so the same Seed always gives the same pattern in every format. Steps run at the host tempo and lock to the song position while the transport plays; Mutate redraws a share of the unlocked lanes per cycle from the song position, so a loop always plays the same variation. Incoming MIDI passes through; the audio passes through untouched.
