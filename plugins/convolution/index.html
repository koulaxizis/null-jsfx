Controls

  • SourceSmall Room / Large Room / Hall / Plate / Cathedral / IR File — Built-in generated IR (Small Room, Large Room, Hall, Plate, Cathedral) or IR File
  • IR FileWAV file in REAPER/Data/null_jsfx_ir — WAV impulse response loaded from REAPER’s Data/null_jsfx_ir folder (used when Source = IR File)
  • Length10 to 100% — Trims the IR length
  • Pre-Delay0 to 200 ms — Shifts the IR later in time
  • Low Cut20 to 1000 Hz — One-pole low cut on the wet signal
  • High Cut1000 to 20000 Hz — One-pole high cut on the wet signal
  • Mix0 to 100% — Dry/wet blend
  • Output−24 to +12 dB — Final output level

Best uses

  • Realistic rooms, halls and plates
  • Load your own IRs of spaces or devices
  • Guitar cabinet IRs via IR File
  • Short ambience on drums and vocals
  • Cathedral-scale tails

Properties

Signal chain: Input → Uniformly Partitioned FFT Convolution (1024-sample blocks) → Low Cut → High Cut → Mix → Output. Latency 1024 samples (reported to REAPER); custom IRs are WAV files placed in REAPER’s Data/null_jsfx_ir folder, resampled, level-normalised and limited to 3 s, stereo IRs per channel.

Formats

  • JSFXJSFX for REAPER, with REAPER's own sliders
  • GFXJSFX GFX for REAPER, with its own interface
  • VST3VST3 for any DAW

  • LV2LV2 for Linux DAWs such as Ardour, Carla and Qtractor
