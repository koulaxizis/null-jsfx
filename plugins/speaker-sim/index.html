SPEAKER SIM

Phone, Laptop and Car Speakers

Download free Part of NULL JSFX 3.0: 150 plugins, free and open source (MIT). Easiest with NULL JSFX Hub.

Controls

  • DevicePhone / Laptop / Earbuds / TV / Radio / Telephone / Megaphone / Car — The device: phone, laptop, earbuds, TV, radio, telephone, megaphone or car.
  • Mix0 to 100% — Blend between the dry signal and the device.
  • Drive0 to 100% — Extra clipping of the small speaker.
  • Output−24 to +12 dB — Output level.

Best uses

  • Checking how a mix translates to phones, laptops and cars (on the monitor FX)
  • Telephone and radio voices
  • Megaphone shouts
  • Lo-fi textures

Properties

Input → mono sum (phone, radio, telephone, megaphone) → 24 dB/oct high-pass → two resonance bands → soft clip tanh(g·x)/g (Drive, plus the megaphone's own) → 24 dB/oct low-pass → Dry/Wet Mix → Output. Zero latency.

Formats

  • JSFXJSFX for REAPER, with REAPER's own sliders
  • GFXJSFX GFX for REAPER, with its own interface
  • VST3VST3 for any DAW
  • CLAPCLAP for any DAW
  • LV2LV2 for Linux DAWs such as Ardour, Carla and Qtractor
