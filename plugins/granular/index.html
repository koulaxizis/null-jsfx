GRANULAR

Clouds of Grains from Your Samples

Download free Part of NULL JSFX 4.2: 174 plugins, free and open source (MIT). Easiest with NULL JSFX Hub.

Controls

  • Sample FileWAV file in REAPER/Data/null_jsfx_samples — The WAV or FLAC file the grains come from (Data/null_jsfx_samples); none plays a built-in tone.
  • Position0 to 100% — Where in the sound the grains are taken from.
  • Spray0 to 100% — How far around Position the grains scatter.
  • Scan−100 to +100 %/s — Moves the position while a key is held, forwards or backwards.
  • Grain Size10 to 500 ms — How long each grain is.
  • Density1 to 100 /s — How many grains each key starts per second.
  • Pitch Spread0 to 12 st — How far each grain's pitch may wander up or down.
  • Stereo Spread0 to 100% — How far the grains spread across the stereo field.
  • Reverse0 to 100% — The chance that a grain plays backwards.
  • Root Key0 to 127 — The key that plays the sound at its own pitch.
  • Attack1 to 5000 ms — How slowly a note fades in.
  • Release1 to 10000 ms — How long a note rings after the key.
  • Velocity0 to 100% — How much the key velocity changes the level.
  • Volume−24 to +12 dB — Output level.

Best uses

  • Ambient pads and textures from any recording
  • Freezing a moment of a voice or an instrument
  • Slowly scanning through a field recording
  • Shimmering, detuned or reversed clouds for sound design

Properties

MIDI → 8 voices → each starts Density grains/s: Hann window of Grain Size, from Position + Scan ± Spray (Park-Miller draws), pitch from Root Key ± Pitch Spread, pan ± Stereo Spread, backwards with chance Reverse; up to 64 grains, linear reading → voice envelope (Attack, Release) → 1/√(density × size) → Volume. Every MIDI message passes on.

Formats
