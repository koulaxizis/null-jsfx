SMOOTHER BETA

One-Knob Harshness Softener

Beta: its rules have been tested on test signals so far and may still be tuned from your feedback, so a project saved now can sound a little different after an update.

Download free Part of NULL JSFX 4.0: 168 plugins, free and open source (MIT). Easiest with NULL JSFX Hub.

Listen

Live: the plugin itself runs in your browser on a loop of NULL JSFX instruments. Change the preset or bypass it while it plays.

Controls

  • Amount0 to 100 — How much harshness to soften: 0 is off; higher values lower the threshold (-14 to -20 dB of a band's share of the whole signal) and deepen the cuts (up to 10 dB) of four bands at 2, 3.15, 5 and 8 kHz. A band is cut only while it pokes out of the mix, by what it rises over the threshold

Best uses

  • Tame a harsh vocal without dulling it
  • Soften cymbals and hi-hats
  • Take the fizz off distorted guitars
  • Polish a bright mix bus or master
  • De-harsh a podcast voice

Properties

Input → mono sum → power envelope (2 ms / 80 ms) → four band-pass detectors (2, 3.15, 5, 8 kHz, Q 2) with their own envelopes → each band's share of the whole signal in dB → cut = (share - threshold) × Amount, up to 10 dB → the four bands taken out of each channel one after another (x - band × (1 - gain)) → Output. Zero latency.
