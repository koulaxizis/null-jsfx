STRUM BETA

Guitar Strums From Held Chords

Beta: its rules have been tested on test signals so far and may still be tuned from your feedback, so a project saved now can sound a little different after an update.

Download free Part of NULL JSFX 4.1.1: 174 plugins, free and open source (MIT). Easiest with NULL JSFX Hub.

Controls

  • DirectionDown / Up / Alternate / Random — Which way the chord is strummed: Down (lowest note first), Up (highest first), Alternate, or Random
  • Speed0 to 100 ms — Time between one string and the next
  • RhythmOnce / 1/4 / 1/8 / 1/16 / Down-Up 1/8 / Folk / Offbeat — Once strums when the chord is played; the others strum again on the tempo while keys are held: 1/4, 1/8, 1/16, Down-Up eighths, Folk (D - D U - U D U) and Offbeat
  • Gate10 to 100% — How long each strum lasts as a share of its step, in the rhythms
  • Velocity Spread0 to 100% — How much softer the last string is than the first
  • Upstroke Velocity10 to 100% — The velocity of up strokes, as a share of the keys' velocity
  • Humanize0 to 20 ms — Moves each string a little later, by up to this many ms
  • Collect0 to 100 ms — Keys pressed within this time of the first one make one chord

Best uses

  • Guitar-like chords from a keyboard into Pluck, Keys or any instrument
  • Strummed rhythm parts on the tempo from held chords
  • Harp rolls and slow arpeggiated chords
  • Pairs with Chords: one finger in, a strummed chord out

Properties

MIDI in → the held keys (collected over Collect) are sorted by pitch and played string by string, Speed apart, lowest first down and highest first up; velocity falls by Velocity Spread over the strings, up strokes at Upstroke Velocity, Humanize by an exact Park-Miller step. Once: one strum per chord, notes held with their keys; rhythms: strums on the tempo, each Gate of its step. A key's note-off ends its note and drops its waiting strings. Other MIDI passes through at its own sample. Audio passes through.
