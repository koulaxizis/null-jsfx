ECHO ONE BETA

One-Knob Tempo Delay

Beta: its rules have been tested on test signals so far and may still be tuned from your feedback, so a project saved now can sound a little different after an update.

Download free Part of NULL JSFX 4.1.1: 174 plugins, free and open source (MIT). Easiest with NULL JSFX Hub.

Listen

Live: the plugin itself runs in your browser on a loop of NULL JSFX instruments. Change the preset or bypass it while it plays.

Controls

  • Amount0 to 100 — Delay amount in one move: 0 to 24 is a soft 1/16 slapback, 25 to 49 a 1/8 echo, 50 to 74 a dotted 1/8 and 75 to 100 a 1/4 note; feedback rises from 12% to 62%, the wet level to 50%, the ping-pong spread from 25 up, and the repeats get darker (low-pass 7 to 3 kHz). The time glides to each new value

Best uses

  • Slapback on vocals and guitars
  • Tempo echoes on leads and synths
  • Ping-pong throws on the end of a phrase
  • Dub-style long repeats
  • Quick delay sketches with one control

Properties

Input → tempo-locked delay per channel (host tempo, 120 bpm without one; the time glides over 80 ms; up to 2 s) → low-pass and 150 Hz high-pass in the loop → feedback, crossing sides by the ping-pong amount → mixed with dry → Output. Zero latency.
