VU METER

VU, PPM and K-System Meter

Download free Part of NULL JSFX 3.0: 150 plugins, free and open source (MIT). Easiest with NULL JSFX Hub.

Controls

  • ModeVU / PPM / K-14 / K-20 — VU (300 ms ballistics, average reading), PPM (EBU Type IIa peak programme meter), K-14 or K-20 (Katz RMS with the 0 mark at -14 or -20 dBFS).
  • Reference−24 to −8 dBFS — The level that reads 0 in VU and PPM: a sine peaking at -24 to -8 dBFS (-18 is the EBU alignment level, -20 SMPTE).
  • Peak HoldOff / 3 s / Hold — How the held maximum (the silver mark) behaves: Off, held for 3 s, or held until you click the meters.
  • MonitorStereo / Mono / Side — Monitor: Stereo, Mono (both channels summed) or Side (the difference only), after the meters.

Best uses

  • Gain staging into analog-modelled plugins at -18 dBFS
  • Mixing to a VU like on a console
  • Broadcast peak levels with an EBU PPM
  • Mastering with the K-14 or K-20 system

Properties

Per channel. VU: the rectified signal through a second-order low pass (damping 0.8: about 1.5 % overshoot, 99 % in 300 ms), sine-calibrated, on the classic -20 to +3 VU dial. PPM: a peak follower charging with a 5 ms time constant (about -4 dB on a 10 ms burst) and falling 24 dB in 2.8 s, -24 to +12 around the reference. K-14 / K-20: RMS over 300 ms, sine-calibrated. OVER lights for 1 s at 0 dBFS. The audio passes through (Monitor folds it after the meters). No latency, no Rack module.
