STEREO SCOPE

Goniometer & Correlation Meter

Download free Part of NULL JSFX 2.5: 113 plugins, free and open source (MIT). Easiest with NULL JSFX Hub.

Controls

  • Scope Gain−12 to +24 dB — Display gain of the goniometer when Auto Gain is off.
  • Auto GainOff / On — Scales the goniometer to the signal level automatically.
  • Decay0 to 100% — Persistence: how much recent history the goniometer shows, newest samples brightest.
  • Integration50 to 3000 ms — Time constant of the correlation, balance and width measurement.
  • MonitorStereo / Mono / Side — Listen to the stereo signal, its mono sum or its side signal (the meter always reads the input).

Best uses

  • Checking mono compatibility and phase problems
  • Judging stereo width and balance while mixing and mastering
  • Spotting out-of-phase or swapped channels
  • Comparing the image of references

Properties

Pass-through (bit-exact in Stereo monitor mode). Integrated <LL>, <RR>, <LR> (one-pole, Integration time) → correlation (-1..+1), balance (dB) and width (side/mid level). Goniometer of the last 8192 samples (L/R rotated 45°), 5 s correlation history, L/R peak meters. Zero latency.

Formats

  • JSFXJSFX for REAPER, with REAPER's own sliders
  • GFXJSFX GFX for REAPER, with its own interface
  • VST3VST3 for any DAW
  • CLAPCLAP for any DAW
