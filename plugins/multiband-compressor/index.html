Controls

  • Crossover 150 to 1000 Hz — Split between Low and LowMid bands (50–1000 Hz).
  • Crossover 2500 to 4000 Hz — Split between LowMid and HighMid bands (500–4000 Hz, kept at least 1.5× above Crossover 1).
  • Crossover 34000 to 12000 Hz — Split between HighMid and High bands (4–12 kHz, kept at least 1.5× above Crossover 2).
  • Low Threshold−60 to 0 dB — Level where the Low band starts compressing.
  • Low Ratio1 to 20 — Compression strength for the Low band.
  • Low Makeup0 to 12 dB — Gain added to the Low band after compression.
  • Low ModeActive / Bypass / Mute / Solo — Active compresses the Low band; Bypass passes it uncompressed (makeup still applies); Mute removes it; Solo plays only soloed bands.
  • LowMid Threshold−60 to 0 dB — Level where the LowMid band starts compressing.
  • LowMid Ratio1 to 20 — Compression strength for the LowMid band.
  • LowMid Makeup0 to 12 dB — Gain added to the LowMid band after compression.
  • LowMid ModeActive / Bypass / Mute / Solo — Active compresses the LowMid band; Bypass passes it uncompressed (makeup still applies); Mute removes it; Solo plays only soloed bands.
  • HighMid Threshold−60 to 0 dB — Level where the HighMid band starts compressing.
  • HighMid Ratio1 to 20 — Compression strength for the HighMid band.
  • HighMid Makeup0 to 12 dB — Gain added to the HighMid band after compression.
  • HighMid ModeActive / Bypass / Mute / Solo — Active compresses the HighMid band; Bypass passes it uncompressed (makeup still applies); Mute removes it; Solo plays only soloed bands.
  • High Threshold−60 to 0 dB — Level where the High band starts compressing.
  • High Ratio1 to 20 — Compression strength for the High band.
  • High Makeup0 to 12 dB — Gain added to the High band after compression.
  • High ModeActive / Bypass / Mute / Solo — Active compresses the High band; Bypass passes it uncompressed (makeup still applies); Mute removes it; Solo plays only soloed bands.
  • Attack0 to 200 ms — How fast gain reduction is applied in all bands.
  • Release0 to 1000 ms — How fast gain recovers in all bands.
  • Knee0 to 100% — Width of the soft knee around each threshold (100% = ±60 dB).
  • Master Output0 to 12 dB — Overall output gain after the bands are summed.
  • ChannelsStereo / Mid / Side — Channels: Stereo works on the whole signal; Mid or Side processes only that half of the mid/side pair and leaves the other untouched.
  • ListenOutput / Delta — Listen: Output plays the result; Delta plays only what each band's compressor takes away, before Makeup and Master Output.

Best uses

  • Mastering bus dynamics
  • Controlling a boomy low end without dulling the top
  • Taming harsh upper mids on vocals or guitars
  • Per-band level balancing with Solo to audition each band
  • Broadcast-style density

Properties

Input → complementary crossovers (four cascaded one-pole low-passes, subtracted from the remainder) → 4 bands, each with stereo-linked peak compression, knee and makeup → Mute/Solo/Bypass → Sum → Master Output → Output. The bands sum back to the original signal with compression off; zero latency.
