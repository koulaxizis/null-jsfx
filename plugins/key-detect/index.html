KEY DETECT BETA

Find the Key of What You Hear

Beta: its rules have been tested on test signals so far and may still be tuned from your feedback, so a project saved now can sound a little different after an update.

Listen

Live: the plugin itself runs in your browser on a loop of NULL JSFX instruments. Change the preset or bypass it while it plays.

Controls

  • Window2 to 60 s — How long the analysis remembers: short follows a key change quickly, long settles on the key of the whole song
  • Threshold−80 to −20 dB — Frames quieter than this are ignored, so silence and noise do not move the result
  • ProfileKrumhansl / Temperley — The key profiles compared with what is heard: Krumhansl-Kessler (from listening tests) or Temperley (from scores)
  • HoldOff / On — Keeps the result as it is

Best uses

  • Finding the key of a sample, a loop or a reference track
  • Setting Key and Scale in Scale and Pitch Correction
  • Harmonic mixing: playing songs in related keys one after another
  • Checking that a vocal, a synth line and a sample agree

Properties

Stereo in → out unchanged. Every 4096 samples the last 16384 go through an FFT; the bins from C2 to C7 fold into 12 pitch classes, with the 3rd and 5th harmonics of lower notes taken away so the key is not pulled towards its dominant. The running chroma is compared with the 24 major and minor key profiles by correlation: the interface shows the key, its confidence, the runner-up, the relative key and the chroma.
