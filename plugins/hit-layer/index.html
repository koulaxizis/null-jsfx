A Tone and Noise Layer Under Every Hit

Controls

  • Threshold−60 to 0 dB — Level a hit must pass to trigger the layer
  • Tone30 to 2000 Hz — Pitch of the tone the layer settles on
  • Tone Level0 to 100% — Level of the tone
  • Tone Decay10 to 2000 ms — Time the tone takes to fade by 60 dB
  • Pitch Drop0 to 24 semitones — How far above Tone the tone starts (it falls to Tone in about a third of Tone Decay)
  • Noise Level0 to 100% — Level of the noise burst
  • Noise Decay5 to 1000 ms — Time the noise takes to fade by 60 dB
  • Noise Color200 to 16000 Hz — Centre of the noise's band-pass (low = body, high = air and wires)
  • Follow0 to 100% — How much the layer follows the strength of each hit (0% = every hit the same)
  • Width0 to 100% — Gives the noise an independent right channel (0% = mono noise)
  • Dry0 to 100% — How much of the input is kept (0% = the layer alone)
  • Output−24 to +12 dB — Output level

Best uses

  • A sub thump under a thin kick
  • Body and wires under a weak snare
  • Boom under toms
  • Air and sizzle under claps and rims

Properties

Signal chain: Input → transient detector (a fast and a slow envelope of the mono sum; a hit is the fast one 6 dB over the slow one above Threshold, then 30 ms before the next) → a sine with a pitch drop and band-passed noise (exact Park-Miller, a state-variable band-pass) → added to the dry signal → Output. Each part fades by 60 dB over its decay; Follow scales the layer with the hit's strength over 24 dB. Stereo, zero latency.
