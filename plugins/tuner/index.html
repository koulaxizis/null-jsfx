TUNER

Chromatic Tuner with Strobe

Download free Part of NULL JSFX 2.7: 126 plugins, free and open source (MIT). Easiest with NULL JSFX Hub.

Controls

  • Reference A4415 to 466 Hz — The pitch of A4 the notes are named from, 415 to 466 Hz (440 standard, 415 baroque, 442 many orchestras).
  • DisplayNeedle / Strobe — Needle: a bar from -50 to +50 cents. Strobe: three bands that stand still in tune and move right when sharp, left when flat, faster the further off.
  • InputStereo / Left / Right — What is tuned: both channels summed, or only the left or right input.
  • OutputThru / Mute — Thru passes the audio; Mute silences it, for tuning on stage.

Best uses

  • Tuning guitars, basses and synths in the session
  • Checking a singer's or a string player's intonation
  • Baroque (A415) and orchestral (A442) tuning
  • Silent tuning on stage with Mute

Properties

YIN pitch detection: the input low passed and taken down to 22 to 24 kHz, analysed about 47 times a second over the last 1024 + up to 960 samples (FFT autocorrelation, cumulative mean normalised difference, first dip below 0.15), refined by parabolas on the difference function at 1, 2, 4, 8 ... periods; within 0.25 cent from 27 Hz to 4.2 kHz on test tones. 25 Hz to about 4.5 kHz; below -70 dBFS RMS counts as silence, and the last note holds for half a second. Readings within 50 cents glide. No latency, no Rack module.
