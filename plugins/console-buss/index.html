CONSOLE BUSS

Console Summing Bus

Download free Part of NULL JSFX 2.9: 137 plugins, free and open source (MIT). Easiest with NULL JSFX Hub.

Controls

  • CharacterClean / Warm / Punchy / Bright — Clean: no emphasis, symmetric saturation. Warm: the lows saturate first, with even harmonics. Punchy: the deep lows saturate first. Bright: the highs saturate first.
  • Drive0 to 100% — How hard the bus is driven (up to 9 or 12 dB depending on the character), and how much the sides leak into each other (up to -30 dB).
  • Output−12 to +12 dB — Output level after the console stage.

Best uses

  • Analog summing colour on the mix bus
  • Pairing with Console Channel on the tracks
  • Gentle density on drum and music busses
  • A touch of desk crosstalk for a less clinical stereo image

Properties

Input → first-order pre-emphasis shelf (per character) → soft saturator (Padé tanh, 8 dB of headroom) → the exact inverse shelf → crosstalk (the other side high-passed at 1 kHz, up to -30 dB with Drive) → 5 Hz DC blocker → Output. Zero latency.

Formats
