Best uses

  • A full salsa, bossa or samba percussion section that follows the song's tempo
  • Shaker and tambourine layers over a pop or rock drum track
  • Switch grooves live from the keyboard by holding C1..G1
  • Play congas, bongos and cowbell by hand from a MIDI keyboard or pads
  • Afrobeat and 12/8 bell patterns for world and film music

Properties

An instrument: MIDI in, stereo out, no samples. Eleven synthesized voices: conga, tumba and bongos as tuned membranes (three inharmonic modes with a pitch drop and a noise band; open, muted and slap strokes), shaker, cabasa and tambourine as filtered noise bursts (with jingles), cowbell from two detuned squares, claves and triangle from resonant modes, guiro from rapid noise ticks. GM-like keys: C4/C#4 bongos, D4 conga mute, D#4 conga open, E4 tumba, F4/F#4 slaps, F#3 tambourine, G#3 cowbell, A4 cabasa, A#4/A#5 shaker, C#5/D5 guiro, D#5 claves, G#5/A5 triangle. Eight built-in grooves synced to the host (or held on C1..G1), with swing, accent and humanize from a deterministic generator; a small stereo room (combs and all-passes). Voices retrigger without clicks.
