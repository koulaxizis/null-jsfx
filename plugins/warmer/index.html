WARMER BETA

One-Knob Analog Warmth

Beta: its rules have been tested on test signals so far and may still be tuned from your feedback, so a project saved now can sound a little different after an update.

Download free Part of NULL JSFX 4.1.1: 174 plugins, free and open source (MIT). Easiest with NULL JSFX Hub.

Listen

Live: the plugin itself runs in your browser on a loop of NULL JSFX instruments. Change the preset or bypass it while it plays.

Controls

  • Amount0 to 100 — Warmth in one move: 0 leaves the signal untouched; higher values drive the lows and low mids (below 600 Hz) into a gentle, slightly asymmetric tape/tube curve (drive x1 to x3, even harmonics) and lift them up to +2.3 dB, ease the top down by up to 2 dB and close a soft low-pass from 20 to 9 kHz, with a small trim so the level stays close to the input

Best uses

  • Warm up a thin vocal or acoustic guitar
  • Round off a DI bass
  • Analog glue on the drum bus
  • A soft glow on the master
  • Take the edge off bright synths

Properties

Input → split at 600 Hz (one pole) → lows: asymmetric tanh curve, DC removed, mixed in by Amount, +0 to +2.3 dB; highs: 0 to -2 dB → one-pole low-pass 20 to 9 kHz faded in by Amount → trim → Output. Zero latency.
