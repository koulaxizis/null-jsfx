LO-FI COMP BETA

Lo-Fi Beat Compressor

Beta: its rules have been tested on test signals so far and may still be tuned from your feedback, so a project saved now can sound a little different after an update.

Listen

Live: the plugin itself runs in your browser on a loop of NULL JSFX instruments. Change the preset or bypass it while it plays.

Controls

  • Squash0 to 100% — How hard it pumps: threshold from -10 to -40 dBFS and ratio from 2:1 to 20:1 together, with its own make-up gain
  • Release20 to 1000 ms — How fast the compressor lets go: short values pump with the beat, long values hold the sound down
  • Sample Rate4000 to 48000 Hz — Sample rate of the old sampler (low-pass and sample-and-hold); at or above the project rate it is off
  • Bits4 to 16 — Bit depth of the quantiser: lower values add grain and noise
  • Wow0 to 100% — Slow pitch drift from two slow LFOs moving a short delay (up to 4 ms)
  • Mix0 to 100% — Blend with the dry signal
  • Output−24 to +12 dB — Output level

Best uses

  • Lo-fi hip hop drums in one effect
  • Pumping, squashed drum busses
  • Dusty sampler sound on keys and loops
  • Wobbly, tape-like pitch drift
  • Parallel crunch with the Mix slider

Properties

Input → stereo-linked peak compressor (1 ms attack, Release, threshold and ratio from Squash, make-up) → short stereo delay read with linear interpolation, moved by two LFOs (0.6 and 0.17 Hz) for wow → one-pole low-pass at 0.45 × Sample Rate and sample-and-hold at Sample Rate (off at or above the project rate) → quantise to Bits → mix with the dry signal → Output gain. No latency reported.
