SPRING REVERB

Vintage Spring Tank

Listen

The same music with the Surf Guitar Drench preset and without the plugin. Switch while it plays.

Controls

  • Decay0.5 to 8 s — Reverb decay time
  • Tension0 to 100% — Strength of the dispersive chirp
  • Tone1000 to 8000 Hz — Darkness of the tank (lowpass in the loop)
  • Drip0 to 100% — Level of the splashy, unfiltered spring echo
  • Pre-Delay0 to 100 ms — Delay before the reverb
  • Mix0 to 100% — Dry/wet blend

Best uses

  • Surf guitar and amp-style reverb
  • Dub drips and boings on snares
  • Vintage character on vocals
  • Lo-fi organ and keys ambience

Properties

Signal chain: Input (summed to mono) → 120 Hz Highpass → Pre-Delay → Two Springs (40-stage dispersive allpass chains in feedback loops, 43.7 / 53.1 ms) → Tone Lowpass + Drip → Mix → Output. Left and right springs differ in length for stereo; zero latency.

Formats

  • JSFXJSFX for REAPER, with REAPER's own sliders
  • GFXJSFX GFX for REAPER, with its own interface
  • VST3VST3 for any DAW
  • CLAPCLAP for any DAW
  • LV2LV2 for Linux DAWs such as Ardour, Carla and Qtractor
