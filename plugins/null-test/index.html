Controls

  • ListenMain / Sidechain / Difference — What you hear: the main, the sidechain lined up with it, or the difference between them
  • AlignManual / Auto — Auto finds the delay and the gain by itself; Manual uses Offset, Trim and Polarity
  • Range1 to 200 ms — How far apart the two may be in time for Auto to find them (up to 200 ms)
  • Offset−10000 to +10000 samples — Manual only: delays the sidechain by this many samples (negative delays the main)
  • Trim−24 to +24 dB — Manual only: the sidechain's gain
  • PolarityNormal / Inverted — Manual only: inverts the sidechain

Best uses

  • Hearing what a plugin really does: its output against its input
  • Checking that two renders, bounces or exports are the same
  • Finding a plugin's latency and gain, or a polarity flip
  • Comparing two compressors or EQs set to match each other

Properties

Main (1/2) and sidechain (3/4) in, stereo out. Auto lines the two up: a phase-transform cross-correlation finds the delay (up to Range, either polarity) and a least-squares fit finds the gain, so a plugin's latency, level change and polarity flip are taken out. The difference is main minus the lined-up sidechain; the interface shows the null depth, the difference in 10 octave bands, the delay, the gain and BIT-IDENTICAL when nothing differs at all.
