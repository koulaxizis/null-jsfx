Release Notes v3.0

November 2026

3.0: 13 NEW PLUGINS

  • DRUMS: Kick (a kick drum synthesizer: pitch sweep, click and drive), Drum Trigger (turns recorded drum hits into MIDI notes, for replacing or layering), Transient Split (sends the attack and the body to different channels) and Euclid (Euclidean rhythms as MIDI)
  • MIDI: Pitch to MIDI (plays a synth from a sung or played line) and Note Echo (repeats notes in time, rising or falling in pitch)
  • SOUND DESIGN: Granular (grain clouds from your own WAV or FLAC files, or a built-in tone), Multi-Tap Delay (up to 8 taps in rhythmic patterns), Bitcrusher and Passive EQ
  • CHECK AND MEASURE: Speaker Sim (hear the mix on a phone, laptop, earbuds or car), Oscilloscope and Test Signal (tones, sweeps, noise and polarity pulses for calibrating speakers)
  • Every one as JSFX, GFX, VST3 / CLAP and LV2, like the rest of the suite

3.0: UPGRADES

  • MPE in Synth, FM, Keys and Pluck: per-note pitch bend, pressure and slide from an MPE controller
  • HARMONIZER: four voices; RESONATOR: chords and MIDI notes; SAMPLER: a slice mode
  • SPECTRUM ANALYZER: a spectrogram and a sidechain overlay; STEREO SCOPE: correlation in three bands; MAXIMIZER: a loudness target with Learn
  • REVERB: a gate and ducking; DELAY: reverse and pitch; PHASER and FLANGER: endless barber-pole sweeps; FREQUENCY SHIFTER: a delay in the feedback
  • FX CHAINS: Ambient Wash, Phone Vocal, and for REAPER Drum Replace and Transient Verb
  • At their default settings the plugins sound exactly as in 2.9

2.9: ASSISTED (BETA)

  • A new category of effects that set themselves: press Learn, play the track for up to 15 seconds, pick what is playing and the sound you want (Natural, Warm, Bright, Punchy, Radio, Podcast, Lo-Fi)
  • ASSISTED EQ: finds the mud, the honk, the harshness and the missing air, and corrects them toward a healthy balance
  • ASSISTED COMP: sets threshold, ratio, attack, release and makeup from the track's levels and rhythm; Podcast brings a voice to -16 LUFS on its own
  • ASSISTED REVERB and ASSISTED DELAY: pre-delay, decay, repeats, tone and level from the track, the delay in time with the project's tempo, both ducking under the dry sound
  • No AI and nothing online: fixed rules from mixing practice, the same result every time. What Learn heard is saved with the project
  • Beta: the rules may still be tuned from your feedback. JSFX, GFX, VST3 / CLAP and LV2, like the rest of the suite

2.8: 7 NEW PLUGINS

  • MIX BUS: Glue Comp (a bus compressor that holds a mix together) and Console Channel with Console Buss (the colour and weight of an analog desk, on the tracks and on the bus they feed)
  • TONE: Air (one slider of open top end) and Tone (bass and treble with movable corners)
  • SPACE: a new reverb with four spaces, Room, Chamber, Hall and Galaxy, from tight rooms to 30-second tails
  • MALLETS: marimba, xylophone, vibraphone, glockenspiel, tubular bells and timpani, with mallet hardness, strike position and the vibraphone's motor and damper
  • Every one written from scratch, as JSFX, GFX, VST3 / CLAP and LV2, like the rest of the suite

2.8: UPGRADES

  • GATE: Tone Release darkens the tail as the gate closes, like a note dying away
  • SATURATION: Focus drives the bass, the mids or the presence harder, and Density thickens the sound
  • PLUCK: Pizzicato violins and cellos
  • ORGAN: a Pipe voice turns the drawbars into the stops of a pipe organ
  • At their default settings the plugins sound exactly as in 2.7

2.7: 13 NEW PLUGINS

  • MIX AND MASTER: Studio EQ (eight bands, mid/side, linear phase), Spectral Ducker (makes room for a vocal in 32 bands), Reference (compare your mix with a commercial track at the same loudness)
  • STUDIO: Tuner, VU Meter, Crossfeed for headphones, De-Clip
  • MIDI: MIDI Utility (transpose, velocity, splits and layers) and MIDI LFO
  • SURROUND: Surround Panner, Downmix Check, Bass Management and Multichannel Loudness for 5.1, 7.1 and 7.1.4 beds
  • Every one as JSFX, GFX, VST3 / CLAP and LV2, like the rest of the suite

2.7: UPGRADES

  • TRUE PEAK: Maximizer and Limiter can limit the true peak, not only the sample peak
  • DELTA: hear only what Maximizer, Limiter, Clipper, Dynamics, Multiband Comp and Dynamic EQ take away
  • MID/SIDE: Dynamics, Multiband Comp, Dynamic EQ and Saturation can work on the mid or the side alone
  • A/B AND UNDO: every interface with presets has undo, redo and two settings to compare
  • OVERSAMPLING in Tape and Ladder Filter; CRACKLE mode in Click Repair; SONG mode and Chance steps in Drum Machine
  • LV2: every plugin also comes as LV2 on Linux
  • MAXIMIZER: with dither on, the VST3, CLAP and LV2 now match the JSFX sample for sample
  • At their default settings the plugins sound exactly as in 2.6.1

INSTALL BY HAND
1

Install Effects

Easiest: NULL JSFX Hub from the Download button installs, updates and removes the plugins in the formats you pick, with the FX chains, presets and extras, and tells you when a new version is out. The installer does a one-off install of the same. Both use the folders below. To do it by hand:

Download JSFX for REAPER and extract the null_jsfx folder to your Reaper Effects directory:

Windows
C:/Users/[Username]/AppData/Roaming/REAPER/Effects/
macOS
~/Library/Application Support/REAPER/Effects/
Linux
~/.config/REAPER/Effects/

Restart Reaper or re-scan your plugins folders. NULL JSFX will be available under the JS category in the FX browser. Keep the gfx folder inside it: it holds the GFX versions (NULL <Name> GFX) and the file that draws their interface.

Presets: copy the files in the presets folder of the download (js-null_jsfx_*.ini) to the presets folder next to Effects (e.g. ~/.config/REAPER/presets/), then pick them from the preset menu at the top of the FX window. If a file with the same name is already there, it holds presets you saved yourself: keep it.

+

VST3 and CLAP (any DAW), LV2 (Linux)

Plugins marked VST3 and CLAP also come as regular plugins. Download VST3 & CLAP for your system and copy the NULL JSFX folders from its VST3 and CLAP folders (on Linux also LV2) to:

Windows
C:/Program Files/Common Files/VST3/ and C:/Program Files/Common Files/CLAP/
macOS
~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/ and ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/CLAP/
Linux
~/.vst3/, ~/.clap/ and ~/.lv2/

Rescan plugins in your DAW. They appear under their own names (e.g. Tilt) with NULL JSFX as the maker.

2

Load Plugins

  1. Click the FX button on any track
  2. Open the FX Browser (if not visible)
  3. Navigate to the JS category
  4. Find plugins under JS: NULL JSFX folder
  5. Select any plugin to load it
💡 Tip: To see all plugins organized in the FX explorer, filter by searching "NULL" or create a Smart Folder: Menu "View" → FX Browser → right-click on "Folders" → "Add folder" → "Smart folder" → Filter contains "NULL". They will auto-categorize!
🎤 Shortcut: Press / to quickly focus the plugin search bar. ESC closes all modals.
